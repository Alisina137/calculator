# Release Assets

## Production app icon
- Master: 1024 × 1024 PNG
- No transparent background for the store master
- Simple calculator identity that remains recognizable at small sizes
- Persian/Dari-first visual language without relying on tiny text

## Android adaptive icon
- Foreground artwork with generous safe-zone padding
- Solid background color
- Keep critical artwork within the central safe area

## Splash screen
- Match the app's light blue-gray visual system
- Center the app mark
- Keep the composition minimal and fast-loading

## Store screenshots
Capture at least:
1. Standard calculator
2. Scientific calculator landscape
3. Everyday tools
4. Percentage or discount calculator
5. Solar Hijri age/date workflow
6. History
7. Persian/Dari + numeral settings

## Release checks
Before wiring assets into app.json:
- export final PNG files;
- confirm exact filenames;
- confirm icon remains legible at 48 × 48;
- confirm splash mark is centered on phone and tablet;
- confirm no text is clipped;
- test both light and dark system launch behavior.
